# Golden Set Human Verification Record

The golden set contains 20 questions with the required 8 Easy / 8 Medium / 4 Hard-Edge distribution. The 16 Easy/Medium questions are designed to be answerable from the corpus; the 4 Hard/Edge questions are deliberately unsupported and are expected to trigger escalation.

**Mandatory learner action before submission:** read every `ideal_answer`, open the cited corpus document(s), and confirm that the wording is supported by the source. The course guide states that ideal answers must be human-written/human-verified and may be spot-checked during DR#1.

Verification checklist:
- [ ] q01–q20 reviewed against the corpus
- [ ] q01–q16 have sufficient source evidence
- [ ] q17–q20 have no supporting source in the corpus
- [ ] Tags and difficulty labels checked
- [ ] No ideal answer relies on external knowledge
