# TechNova Solutions — Employee Benefits Guide
## Overview
TechNova provides eligible employees with a range of benefits intended to support health, financial security, professional development, and work-life balance. Actual eligibility and coverage depend on employment status, work location, plan terms, and applicable law.

## Health and Wellness
Eligible employees may participate in the health plan applicable to their country or employment arrangement. Coverage can include medical, dental, or vision benefits where those plans are offered. Employees should consult the applicable benefits portal for enrollment windows, dependents, and coverage details.

## Retirement and Savings
Eligible employees may participate in company-sponsored retirement or savings programs where available. Contribution rules, company matching, vesting, and tax treatment vary by location and plan.

## Professional Development
Employees may request reimbursement or company funding for approved professional-development activities. Examples include relevant certifications, conferences, technical courses, and role-related training. Approval is normally required before committing company funds.

## Employee Assistance
Where available, employee assistance resources may provide confidential support for personal or workplace challenges. Availability varies by location.

## Enrollment
Newly eligible employees must complete enrollment within the applicable enrollment window. Some benefits may be automatic while others require an employee election.

## Dependents
Dependent eligibility varies by plan. Employees adding dependents may be required to provide documentation within the enrollment period.

## Changes in Status
Marriage, birth or adoption, divorce, loss of other coverage, or other qualifying events may permit a benefits change outside the normal enrollment cycle. Employees should notify HR promptly because deadlines may apply.

## Leaving the Company
Benefits generally end or change when employment ends according to the relevant plan rules. Some programs may provide continuation rights or conversion options where required by law or plan terms.

## Questions
Employees should use the benefits portal or contact HR for plan-specific questions. The benefits guide is a general summary and does not replace official plan documents.

## Relationship to Other Policies
Benefit questions should be distinguished from payroll, leave, and expense questions. The Employee Handbook provides general guidance, while official plan documents control benefit eligibility and coverage.

## Enrollment Decisions
Employees should review plan descriptions before selecting coverage. Some elections can only be made during defined enrollment windows or after qualifying life events. Missing an enrollment deadline may limit the ability to make a change until the next permitted window.

## Payroll and Contributions
Where employee contributions apply, deductions may be taken through payroll according to the applicable plan. Employees should review payslips and benefits records and raise discrepancies with HR or the benefits administrator.

## Professional Development Approval
Training must be relevant to the employee's role or an agreed development objective. Employees should obtain approval before registering for a course or certification if reimbursement is expected. Completion records and receipts may be required.

## Benefits and Leave
Benefits and leave are related but governed by different rules. Taking PTO does not automatically change benefit eligibility. Employees should consult HR for questions involving extended leave, employment status, or qualifying events.

## Local Variation
Because benefit plans differ by country, employees should use the plan documentation applicable to their employment location. A benefit available in one country should not be assumed to be available in another.

## Practical Guidance
The benefits portal and official plan documents should be treated as the primary sources for individual coverage. HR can explain enrollment processes but cannot override the formal terms of an insurance or retirement plan.
