# TechNova Solutions — Data Privacy Policy
## Purpose
TechNova processes personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable privacy laws and contractual requirements.

## Personal Data
Personal data includes information that can identify or relate to an individual, such as name, contact details, employee identifier, location information, or other information defined as personal data by applicable law.

## Purpose Limitation
Personal data should be collected and used for legitimate, defined purposes. Employees must not reuse personal data for unrelated purposes without appropriate authorization.

## Data Minimization
Only the personal information necessary for a legitimate business purpose should be collected or retained.

## Access
Access to personal data should be limited to authorized personnel with a legitimate business need. Employees must protect personal data from unauthorized disclosure.

## Sharing
Personal data may be shared with approved vendors or internal teams when there is a legitimate purpose and required contractual, security, and privacy review.

## Retention
Personal data should not be retained indefinitely. Retention follows applicable schedules, legal requirements, contracts, and business needs.

## Data Subject Requests
Requests from individuals concerning their personal data should be directed to the designated Privacy or HR process rather than handled independently by employees.

## Incidents
Accidental disclosure, loss, unauthorized access, or other privacy incidents must be reported promptly through the approved incident process.

## International Transfers
Cross-border transfers of personal data may require additional legal and contractual safeguards. Employees should consult Privacy before transferring personal data to another country or unapproved system.

## AI and External Tools
Employees must not enter personal or confidential company information into public AI or external services unless the tool has been approved for that data type and use case.

## Examples
An employee should not export an employee contact list to a personal cloud account. A vendor receiving employee data must have appropriate approval and contractual safeguards. A data-subject request should be routed to the designated process rather than answered informally.

This policy works with Information Security, Acceptable Use, and Supplier Management.

## Privacy by Design
Teams should consider privacy requirements early when designing a process, application, report, or automation that uses personal information. Collecting data first and deciding how to protect it later increases risk.

## Accuracy
Where personal information is used for important business purposes, reasonable steps should be taken to keep it accurate and current. Incorrect information should be corrected through the appropriate process.

## Vendors
Before sharing personal information with a supplier, the business owner should confirm that the supplier is approved and that required privacy, security, and contractual controls are in place.

## Automation and AI
Automated processing involving personal data should be reviewed when required by privacy or security procedures. Public AI services should not receive personal data unless explicitly approved for that data type and purpose.

## Breach Response
Employees should report suspected privacy incidents promptly, including accidental emails, exposed files, lost devices, or unauthorized access. They should not wait until they know whether the event is legally a breach.

## Retention and Disposal
When data reaches the end of its required retention period, it should be securely deleted or disposed of according to the applicable schedule.

## Practical Guidance
The key principles are purpose limitation, minimization, controlled access, appropriate sharing, and prompt incident reporting. When uncertain, consult Privacy before transferring or repurposing personal data.
