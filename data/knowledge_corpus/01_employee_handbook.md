# TechNova Solutions — Employee Handbook
## Purpose
This handbook explains the general employment practices, workplace expectations, and employee support principles of TechNova Solutions. It applies to regular full-time and part-time employees unless a local law, employment agreement, or more specific policy provides a different requirement.

## Working Hours and Attendance
The standard working week is Monday through Friday, with normal business hours of 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. local time. Teams may establish different coverage hours when required by customer commitments or operational needs. Employees should maintain accurate attendance and communicate planned absences to their manager as early as possible.

Employees working flexible schedules are expected to remain available during agreed core collaboration hours. Core hours are normally 11:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. local time. Managers may approve alternative arrangements for teams operating across time zones.

## Workplace Conduct
TechNova expects employees to behave respectfully, professionally, and ethically. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, retaliation, and abusive conduct are prohibited. Employees should raise concerns through their manager, Human Resources, the Ethics channel, or another reporting mechanism described in the Code of Conduct.

Employees must protect confidential company information. Information obtained through employment should only be accessed, used, or shared for legitimate business purposes. Employees must follow the Information Security, Data Privacy, Acceptable Use, and Access Management policies.

## Communication and Collaboration
Employees should use company-approved collaboration tools for business information. Business decisions that affect projects should be documented in the appropriate project workspace. Employees are expected to respond to reasonable work communications during their agreed working hours.

## Performance and Development
Employees participate in an annual performance review cycle and may also receive periodic check-ins. Managers are expected to provide meaningful feedback, identify development opportunities, and address performance concerns promptly. Employees are encouraged to maintain an individual development plan.

## Leave
Employees receive paid time off according to the Leave and PTO Policy. Planned leave should be requested through the HR system and approved by the employee's manager before travel or other commitments are made. Emergency or unexpected absence should be communicated to the manager as soon as practical.

## Benefits
Eligible employees may participate in company benefit programs described in the Employee Benefits Policy. Eligibility can depend on employment status, location, and applicable plan terms.

## Remote Work
Remote work may be available when the employee's role, location, security requirements, and manager approval permit it. Remote employees remain subject to the same performance, confidentiality, availability, and security expectations as office-based employees.

## Travel and Expenses
Business travel must be approved in accordance with the Travel and Expense Policy. Employees should use approved booking channels where required and submit eligible expenses with supporting documentation within the specified timeframe.

## Raising Concerns
Employees are encouraged to raise concerns early. Retaliation against an employee who makes a good-faith report or participates in an investigation is prohibited. Where a specialized policy exists, employees should follow that policy for detailed requirements.

## Acknowledgement
Employees are responsible for reading the policies relevant to their work. Where this handbook conflicts with a more specific policy, the more specific policy governs the subject it addresses. Local legal requirements and employment agreements also take precedence where applicable.
