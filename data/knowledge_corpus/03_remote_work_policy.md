# TechNova Solutions — Remote Work Policy
## Purpose
The Remote Work Policy establishes the conditions under which employees may work away from a designated company office.

## Eligibility
Remote work is available only where the employee's role can be performed effectively outside the office and where customer, regulatory, security, and operational requirements permit it. Eligibility does not create an automatic right to work remotely.

Managers assess requests based on role requirements, team coverage, performance, collaboration needs, information-security considerations, and local employment requirements.

## Approval
A regular remote-work arrangement requires manager approval and, where applicable, HR or department approval. The approved work location should be recorded through the company's designated process.

## Temporary Remote Work
Employees may occasionally work from another approved location for a short period with manager approval. Temporary arrangements should not create payroll, immigration, tax, data-residency, or security obligations that the company has not reviewed.

## Working Across Countries
Employees must not work from another country without prior written approval from HR and the appropriate business or compliance function. International remote work can create tax, employment, immigration, data-protection, and permanent-establishment considerations.

Approval for international remote work is therefore not automatic even when the employee's manager agrees.

## Security Requirements
Remote employees must use company-approved devices and security controls. Public computers must not be used for confidential company work. Employees should use approved secure network access, protect screens from unauthorized viewing, and avoid discussing confidential matters in public locations.

Company information should not be downloaded to personal devices unless specifically authorized.

## Availability
Remote employees must follow their agreed working schedule and remain reachable through approved communication tools during core collaboration hours. Remote work does not reduce performance or service expectations.

## Equipment
The company may provide equipment based on role and location. Employees are responsible for reasonable care of company equipment and must report loss, theft, or suspected compromise promptly.

## Review and Termination
A manager may review a remote arrangement when business requirements, performance, security, or team coverage changes. The company may require an employee to return to an office-based arrangement when operationally necessary, subject to applicable law and employment terms.

## Examples
A manager may approve working from home within the employee's approved country. A request to work for three weeks from another country requires additional review even if the employee will work the same hours. An employee cannot use a personal laptop for confidential company work merely because the laptop is convenient.

This policy works with the Information Security, Data Privacy, and Acceptable Use policies.

## Work Environment
Employees working remotely should maintain a workspace that supports confidentiality, productivity, and professional communication. Confidential discussions should not be conducted where unauthorized people can overhear them. Paper documents containing confidential information should be secured and disposed of through approved methods.

## Business Continuity
Remote employees should maintain reliable connectivity appropriate to their role and have a practical plan for handling short service interruptions. If connectivity or equipment issues prevent work, the employee should notify the manager and use the IT support process where appropriate.

## Customer and Regulatory Restrictions
Some customer engagements or regulated activities may require work from a designated location or company facility. These restrictions can override a general remote-work arrangement. Employees should verify project-specific requirements before changing location.

## Expense Considerations
Remote work does not automatically make home-office purchases reimbursable. Equipment or expenses must follow applicable company approval and expense processes.

## Review Criteria
Managers may review remote arrangements based on performance, customer commitments, collaboration, security, or operational coverage. Employees should treat an approved arrangement as conditional on continued compliance with this policy.

## Practical Guidance
The safest approach is to obtain written approval before changing the location from which work is performed. International location changes require the additional HR and compliance review described above.
