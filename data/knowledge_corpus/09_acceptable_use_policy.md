# TechNova Solutions — Acceptable Use Policy
## Purpose
Company technology and networks are provided primarily for legitimate business activities. Limited personal use is permitted when it does not interfere with work, violate policy, create security risk, or consume unreasonable resources.

## Prohibited Activities
Employees must not use company systems for unlawful activity, harassment, unauthorized access, distribution of malicious software, credential theft, circumvention of security controls, or inappropriate content.

Employees must not use company resources to operate outside businesses or conduct commercial activities unrelated to TechNova unless explicitly approved.

## Internet and Email
Employees should exercise caution when visiting websites or opening attachments. Company email must not be used to send confidential information to unauthorized recipients.

## Social Media
Employees may use social media personally, but they must not disclose confidential information or imply that personal opinions represent official company positions unless authorized.

## Software Installation
Employees should install software only through approved processes. Unauthorized applications may create security and licensing risks.

## AI Tools
Generative AI tools may be used for approved business purposes. Employees must follow the Information Security and Data Privacy policies when entering company information into AI systems.

## Monitoring
Company systems may be monitored, logged, or reviewed for security, compliance, operational, and legal purposes, subject to applicable law.

## Personal Use
Reasonable personal use, such as occasional personal browsing during breaks, may be permitted. Personal use must not involve illegal activity, excessive bandwidth, risky downloads, or activities that could damage the company's reputation.

## Enforcement
Violations may result in removal of access, disciplinary action, or other consequences consistent with company policy and applicable law.

## Examples
Checking personal email during a break may be acceptable. Installing an unapproved browser extension that captures credentials is not. Using a company laptop to run a separate commercial business is not permitted without authorization.

Employees should ask IT or Security when uncertain whether a technology use is acceptable.

## Business Resources
Company systems include laptops, mobile devices, networks, email, collaboration platforms, cloud services, and approved applications. The same acceptable-use principles apply across these resources.

## Security Boundaries
Employees must not bypass security controls simply to make a task easier. Examples include disabling endpoint protection, sharing authentication factors, using another employee's credentials, or installing tools that evade monitoring.

## Intellectual Property
Employees must respect company, customer, and third-party intellectual-property rights. Software, documents, images, datasets, and other materials should be used according to applicable licenses and permissions.

## Generative AI
AI-generated output must be reviewed before being used for business decisions or external communication. Employees remain responsible for accuracy, confidentiality, copyright considerations, and compliance with approved-use requirements.

## Monitoring and Privacy
Employees should understand that company systems can be logged or monitored for legitimate business, security, compliance, and legal purposes. Local law may impose additional requirements.

## Reporting
Questions about permitted technology use should be directed to IT or Security. Suspected misuse should be reported through the appropriate channel.

## Practical Guidance
Limited personal use is a privilege subject to safeguards. If an activity introduces meaningful security, legal, licensing, or reputational risk, it should not be performed without explicit approval.
