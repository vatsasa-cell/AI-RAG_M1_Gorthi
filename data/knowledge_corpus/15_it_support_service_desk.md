# TechNova Solutions — IT Support and Service Desk Guide
## Purpose
The Service Desk is the primary support channel for employee technology issues, requests, and incidents.

## How to Raise a Ticket
Employees should use the approved IT service portal for normal requests and incidents. Tickets should include a concise description, business impact, affected device or application, relevant error messages, and steps already attempted.

## Priority
Priority is based on business impact and urgency. A company-wide outage or critical business service interruption may receive the highest priority. A single-user issue with a workaround normally receives a lower priority.

## Security Incidents
Suspected phishing, credential compromise, malware, lost company devices, or unauthorized access should be reported through the security incident mechanism rather than treated as ordinary low-priority IT requests.

## Password Issues
Employees should use the approved password-reset process. Passwords must never be sent to Service Desk personnel by email or chat.

## Equipment
Hardware failures should be reported with the asset identifier where available. Employees should not attempt repairs that could damage company equipment or invalidate support arrangements.

## Software Requests
New software requests require business justification and may require licensing, security, privacy, or procurement review.

## Remote Support
Service Desk personnel may request approved remote-support access when needed. Employees should verify that they are interacting with legitimate company support personnel before granting access.

## Escalation
Tickets may be escalated to specialist teams when the Service Desk cannot resolve them. Employees should keep the ticket updated if business impact changes.

## Service Expectations
Target response and resolution times depend on ticket priority and service-level commitments. A response target is not necessarily the same as a final resolution target.

## Examples
A user unable to print a non-critical document should normally use the IT service portal. A suspected stolen laptop should be reported immediately through the security process. A request for a new licensed application may require IT and Procurement review.

This guide should be used with Information Security, Access Management, and Acceptable Use policies.

## Ticket Quality
A useful ticket explains what happened, when it started, who is affected, the business impact, and what troubleshooting has already been attempted. Screenshots or error messages can help when they do not expose sensitive information.

## Priority Changes
Employees should update the ticket when business impact increases. For example, an issue affecting one user may become critical if it prevents an entire customer-facing operation from functioning.

## Security Escalation
Employees should not place passwords, authentication codes, or sensitive personal information into an ordinary support ticket unless an approved secure mechanism specifically requires it.

## Remote Support
Remote-support sessions should use company-approved tools. Employees should verify the support request before granting access and should end sessions when assistance is complete.

## Software and Access
Requests for new software or system access may require approvals from managers, Security, Privacy, Procurement, or system owners. Service Desk cannot independently bypass these controls.

## Knowledge and Self-Service
Employees should use approved knowledge articles and self-service tools for common tasks such as password resets. Following the standard process helps reduce delays and creates consistent records.

## Escalation and Closure
When a specialist team resolves an issue, the Service Desk may confirm the solution with the employee before closure. Employees should reopen or update the ticket if the problem returns.

## Practical Guidance
Use the normal service portal for ordinary technology problems and the security incident mechanism for security-sensitive events. When in doubt, report promptly and let the support or security team route the issue appropriately.
