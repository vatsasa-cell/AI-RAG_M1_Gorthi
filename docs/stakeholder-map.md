# Stakeholder Map

The personas are fictional and designed to represent distinct EKA usage patterns.

## 1. Priya Nair — HR Generalist
- **Role and experience:** HR Generalist; 5 years' experience.
- **Current workflow:** Searches policy folders and the intranet, then responds to employee questions or asks an HR policy owner when wording is unclear.
- **EKA usage pattern:** Several times per week, often while assisting an employee; mainly leave, benefits, remote-work, and performance-policy questions.
- **Benefit if EKA succeeds:** Faster first-line answers with source citations; less time spent locating the correct policy version.

## 2. Arjun Mehta — Software Engineer
- **Role and experience:** Software Engineer; 3 years' experience.
- **Current workflow:** Uses the IT portal and messages colleagues when access, security, software, or support procedures are unclear.
- **EKA usage pattern:** Short, task-oriented questions about passwords, access, security incidents, software use, and IT support.
- **Benefit if EKA succeeds:** Immediate self-service answers and clearer escalation paths for security-sensitive issues.

## 3. Kavita Rao — Procurement Specialist
- **Role and experience:** Procurement Specialist; 7 years' experience.
- **Current workflow:** Checks procurement rules, supplier records, and approval requirements; escalates unusual supplier or spend cases to Procurement/Legal.
- **EKA usage pattern:** Several times per week; asks approval-threshold, supplier-onboarding, and policy-cross-reference questions.
- **Benefit if EKA succeeds:** Faster retrieval of authoritative policy passages while retaining source traceability.

## 4. Daniel Thomas — Operations Manager
- **Role and experience:** Operations Manager; 8 years' experience.
- **Current workflow:** Answers team questions using remembered process knowledge, internal documents, and escalation to specialist functions.
- **EKA usage pattern:** Occasional use for policy/process confirmation and cross-document questions before advising the team.
- **Benefit if EKA succeeds:** More consistent guidance and fewer interruptions for routine questions.

## 5. Meera Shah — Compliance & Privacy Partner
- **Role and experience:** Compliance/Privacy Partner; 9 years' experience.
- **Current workflow:** Reviews higher-risk questions, vendor/data scenarios, and exceptions; requires traceability to source material.
- **EKA usage pattern:** Lower volume but higher scrutiny; checks citations and expects the assistant to escalate when evidence is missing.
- **Benefit if EKA succeeds:** Better self-service for routine questions while preserving human review for ambiguous or high-risk cases.

## Key Design Tension

Priya and Arjun prioritize speed and low effort; Meera and Kavita prioritize source traceability and cautious escalation. The design therefore treats **grounded citations and honest refusal as first-class requirements**, not optional enhancements.
